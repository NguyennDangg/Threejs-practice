01 / APPROACH

The observer
begins at rest.

Nothing moves until intent is expressed. Scroll is intent.

02 / INSPECTION

Distance is
a choice.

Approach without commitment. The subject permits scrutiny.

03 / PASSAGE

The axis
inverts.

To study the whole, abandon the front. The reverse is also true.

04 / DISPERSAL

Cohesion
releases.

Each element holds its own position. Order becomes a proposal.

05 / RETURN

The cycle
closes.

What began at rest returns to rest. Changed, not travelled.
